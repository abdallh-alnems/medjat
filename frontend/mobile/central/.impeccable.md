# Medjat Central — Design Notes

## Design Context

### Users
Business owners, HR staff, and branch managers of small/medium companies, viewing
on **mobile** (Flutter app, **RTL Arabic** primary). They check the home dashboard
to glance at today's attendance, pending approvals, and payroll/branch health —
often quickly, between other tasks.

### Brand Personality
Calm, trustworthy, organized. A management tool that should feel **clear and
unhurried**, never noisy. Confidence over flash.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined, quiet, well-structured. Clear visual hierarchy built mostly from **space
and weight**, with the brand color used sparingly as an accent (60-30-10). Light,
data-aware product UI. NOT a flashy marketing dashboard, NOT card-grid monotony,
NOT hero-metric gradients.

### Design System (existing — reuse, don't reinvent)
- **Spacing**: `AppSpacing` 4pt scale — s1=4, s2=8, s3=12, s4=16, s5=24, s6=32, s7=48, s8=64, s9=96.
- **Radius**: `AppRadius` sm=6, md=10, lg=16, full=999.
- **Colors**: `AppColors.of(context)` → canvas, surface, sunken, borderHairline,
  borderStrong, textPrimary/Secondary/Tertiary, brand, brandHover, brandSubtle,
  accentWarm, error, warning, success.
- **Type**: body/labels `IBM Plex Sans Arabic`; numbers/metrics `Geist`.
  Helpers: `AppTextStyles.h2/h3/sm`.
- **Motion**: `AppMotion` easeOutQuart, 320ms.

### Design Principles
1. **Hierarchy through space, not boxes** — group related stats; separate sections
   with generous whitespace (s6+). Avoid wrapping everything in cards.
2. **Consistent card sizing** — within a group, cards share one size/density.
   Don't mix big and small cards in the same row.
3. **Quiet color** — neutral surfaces dominate; brand/status colors only on the
   data they describe.
4. **No AI tells** — no left/right accent stripes, no gradient text, no decorative
   sparklines, no nested cards.
5. **Glanceable** — the most important "today" numbers readable in under 2 seconds.
