# Permedjat — Design Context

> Design system foundation for the Permedjat HR SaaS platform (3 Flutter apps: Employee / Admin / Super Admin). This document is the single source of truth for all design decisions.

---

## Design Context

### Users

**Primary audience:** Egyptian and North African SMB workforce — three distinct user groups across three apps:

- **Employees** (Mobile, daily use): Factory workers, office staff, retail clerks, technicians. Tech literacy varies wildly — from 22-year-old graduates to 55-year-old foremen. Used **twice a day on average** to check in/out. Often in non-ideal contexts: bright sunlight at a factory gate, low light in a warehouse, unstable internet.
- **Admin/HR/Managers** (Mobile, multiple times daily): Owners, HR officers, branch managers. Need density without overwhelm. Make consequential decisions (approving leaves, issuing payroll). Typically in offices or on-the-go.
- **Super Admins** (Mobile, infrequent): Internal team only. Operational/technical screens — function over form.

**Context of use:**
- Arabic-first, RTL throughout
- Egyptian and MENA workplace environments — formal hierarchy matters
- Often outdoors or in noisy environments (factory floor, retail store)
- Mix of high-end iPhones (HR/Owners) and budget Android (employees)
- Frequent offline scenarios — must feel reliable when disconnected

**Job to be done:**
- Employees: "Confirm I was here, and verify I'm being paid correctly."
- Admins: "Run payroll and attendance with confidence — without errors."
- The product replaces Excel sheets and paper. It must feel **more trustworthy** than the spreadsheet it replaces.

---

### Brand Personality

**Three words:** **رصين، دقيق، موثوق** — *Composed, Precise, Trustworthy.*

**Voice:** A senior accountant who has been in the industry 20 years. Doesn't oversell. Speaks plainly. Doesn't use slang or exclamation marks. When something goes wrong, says it directly without alarming the user. When something goes right, doesn't celebrate excessively — just confirms it cleanly.

**Emotional goal:** Quiet confidence. The user should close the app and forget about it — that's success. This is **infrastructure**, not entertainment. No employee should ever pause and admire the UI. They should check in and move on with their day. The admin should run payroll and trust the number.

**Anti-personality:**
- ❌ Not playful, friendly, or chatty
- ❌ Not "modern startup-y" with bright illustrations
- ❌ Not corporate-cold either — there is warmth, but it's understated
- ❌ Not "fintech bro" with neon accents and dark mode flexing

---

### Aesthetic Direction

**Direction:** **Editorial-Utility** — the design vocabulary of a high-end financial publication (Bloomberg Terminal's precision + The Economist's restraint), translated to a mobile workforce tool. Think: a stainless steel watch, not a smartwatch. Precision instruments, not consumer electronics.

**Visual tone:**
- **Restrained, almost austere** — surfaces stay calm, content does the talking
- **Information-dense but legible** — numbers and times are the heroes; never hide them behind decoration
- **Asymmetric, editorial layouts** — never centered "marketing page" feel; favors a strong left rail (in RTL: right rail) with content flowing from it
- **Generous breathing room around critical numbers** — the salary figure, the check-in time, the deduction total each get their own space
- **Hairline borders over heavy shadows** — 1px dividers, not card drop-shadows
- **No emoji in the UI.** Custom icon set only (line icons, 1.5px stroke).

**Theme:**
- **Light mode is the default** (employees use it outdoors and in offices during daylight)
- **Dark mode available with toggle** (preferred by night-shift workers and managers checking late)
- Both themes share the same color identity — dark is a true companion mode, not "light mode inverted"

**Brand color:**
- **Deep Teal** — `oklch(0.50 0.10 200)` as the primary. Not bright cyan, not turquoise pool. The teal of a 1960s mechanical pencil, an oxidized copper rooftop, deep water. Carries the trust association of blue without the over-used SaaS-blue cliché.
- **Tinted neutrals** — every gray is shifted toward hue 200 by a chroma of 0.005-0.01. Cohesion is invisible but felt.
- **Single warm accent** — a muted ochre `oklch(0.70 0.13 75)` used **sparingly** for positive financial values (bonuses, paid status). Never for decoration.
- **Functional reds and yellows** — only for errors, warnings, late status. Never for delight or branding.

**Typography:**
- **Arabic:** **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** — engineered precision, exceptional clarity at small sizes (critical for time stamps and numbers in employee app). Pairs naturally with its Latin counterpart.
- **Latin & Numerals:** **Geist** (Vercel) — modern grotesque with excellent **tabular numerals**, essential for aligning salary figures and times in tables.
- **Heading-to-body contrast:** Use weight (700 for headings, 400 for body) and size (1.333 modular ratio) — not different font families.
- **Numerals use `font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1`** everywhere financial data appears.

**Anti-references** (explicitly avoided):
- ❌ Gusto / BambooHR illustration-heavy aesthetic
- ❌ Fintech "neon on black" (Robinhood, vintage Coinbase)
- ❌ Generic Bootstrap-y dashboards with side-stripe alerts
- ❌ Material Design "every container is a card with shadow"
- ❌ Glassmorphism, blur, gradient text, gradient backgrounds
- ❌ Rounded illustrations of smiling people
- ❌ "Wellness app" pastels

### Design Principles

These principles override individual preferences. When a decision is unclear, return to these:

1. **الرقم هو البطل** — *The number is the hero.*
   Every screen has one primary number (check-in time, net salary, hours worked). It gets the largest size, tabular alignment, and surrounding space. Decoration never competes with data.

2. **الصمت أبلغ** — *Silence speaks louder.*
   Empty space is a feature, not a waste. Resist filling. A screen with one number and a quiet caption beats a screen with five widgets.

3. **الحد الأدنى من الألوان** — *Color is rationed.*
   60% neutral surfaces, 30% text and structural lines, 10% color total. The brand teal appears only where action or identity is needed. Warm accent appears only on positive monetary values.

4. **الحدود لا الظلال** — *Borders, not shadows.*
   1px hairlines (`oklch(0.92 0.005 200)` light / `oklch(0.28 0.008 200)` dark) define structure. Drop shadows reserved for modals/overlays only. No card has a shadow by default.

5. **الأرقام تصطف** — *Numbers align.*
   Tabular numerals everywhere. Right-aligned in RTL when columnar. Currency symbol consistent position. Time always formatted `٨:٠٢ ص` (with Eastern Arabic numerals as an option) or `8:02 AM` (Western), never mixed within a screen.

6. **الإيقاع لا التكرار** — *Rhythm, not repetition.*
   Vary section spacing intentionally. The header has more space below it than a list item. A critical action has more room around it than a passive label. Reject the templated "padding: 16px everywhere" reflex.

7. **الـ Offline حالة كاملة** — *Offline is a first-class state.*
   Every offline indicator is part of the design language, not a tacked-on warning. Pending sync items have their own quiet visual treatment — a thin marker (not a yellow stripe), tabular and informative.

## Typography Scale

Fixed `rem` scale (mobile app — not fluid). Modular ratio: **1.333** (perfect fourth).

| Token | Size | Weight | Usage |
|-------|------|--------|-------|
| `--text-display` | 2.25rem (36px) | 700 | Hero numbers (net salary, check-in time) |
| `--text-h1` | 1.75rem (28px) | 700 | Screen titles |
| `--text-h2` | 1.375rem (22px) | 600 | Section headers |
| `--text-h3` | 1.125rem (18px) | 600 | Card titles |
| `--text-body` | 1rem (16px) | 400 | Body, list items |
| `--text-sm` | 0.875rem (14px) | 400 | Meta, captions |
| `--text-xs` | 0.75rem (12px) | 500 | Labels, eyebrow text |

**Line heights:** 1.2 for display/h1, 1.35 for h2/h3, 1.55 for body, 1.5 for sm/xs.

**Letter-spacing:**
- `--text-xs` labels: `+0.04em` (improves legibility at small sizes, gives "label" feeling)
- Display numbers: `-0.02em` (tightens hero figures)
- Body: 0

## Spacing Scale (4pt)

```
--space-1: 4px
--space-2: 8px
--space-3: 12px
--space-4: 16px
--space-5: 24px
--space-6: 32px
--space-7: 48px
--space-8: 64px
--space-9: 96px
```

**Heuristics:**
- Between related items in a list: `--space-3` (12px)
- Between unrelated sections: `--space-6` to `--space-7`
- Around hero numbers: `--space-7` minimum
- Card internal padding: `--space-5` (24px)
- Screen edge padding: `--space-4` (16px) mobile

## Component Principles

- **Buttons:** No drop shadow. Primary = filled brand. Secondary = hairline border. Tertiary = text-only with `--brand` color.
- **Inputs:** Hairline border `--border-hairline`. On focus: border becomes `--brand`, no glow, no blue ring. Label sits **above** the field (not floating).
- **Cards:** No shadow. Hairline border + `--bg-surface`. Internal padding `--space-5`. Avoid nesting cards.
- **Lists:** Hairline dividers between rows. Row height min 56px on mobile (touch target).
- **Status indicators:** Small dots (8px) with semantic color, not pills or badges. Text label follows.
- **Modals:** Reserve for destructive confirmations only. Otherwise use bottom sheets or inline expansion.
- **Bottom Navigation:** No labels at rest, labels on selected only. Hairline top border. 56px height.

---

## Motion

- **Default easing:** `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` (ease-out-quart)
- **Duration:** 200ms for micro-interactions, 320ms for screen transitions
- **No bounce, no elastic, no overshoot**
- **Page transitions:** subtle horizontal slide (16px) + opacity, not full slide
- **Success states:** a single brief check mark animation (200ms) — no confetti, no celebrations
- **Respect `MediaQuery.disableAnimations`** in Flutter

## What This Looks Like in Practice

**Home screen (Employee app):**
A tall, calm screen. Date in `--text-sm` at top. Then enormous breathing room. Then the user's check-in time in `--text-display` (or the action button if not yet checked in). Below: branch name in `--text-secondary`. Nothing else. No card. No background gradient. The status is the screen.

**Payroll detail (Employee app):**
Top: the month name in `--text-h2`. A hairline divider. Then aligned rows: salary base / deductions list / bonuses list / hairline / **net salary in `--text-display`**, right-aligned with tabular numerals. A single text button at the bottom: "تحميل PDF". No card wraps any of this.

**Dashboard (Admin app):**
Three stacked sections separated by `--space-7`. Each section: a small label in `--text-xs` letter-spaced, then a hero number in `--text-display`. No icons. No sparklines as decoration. If a chart appears, it's the focus of the section, not garnish.
