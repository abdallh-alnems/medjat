Clocking in

Three ways to clock in. You choose.

Every company works differently. Pick what fits the branch or the team, and the app handles the rest.

QR + geofence

The employee scans the branch code, and the app confirms in the same moment that they are inside the branch geofence.

Geofence only

One tap to clock in while inside the branch geofence. No code, no extra step.

Manual

A manager records attendance directly — for field teams, and for the days when scanning or GPS will not do.

Set the method for the whole company, for one branch, for a department, or for a single employee — the most specific rule wins.

Privacy

Location is read at the moment of clocking in. Not before, not after.

GPS confirms that an employee is inside their branch geofence when they clock in or out — and only then. We do not track location in the background, and we keep no trail of their movement.

Each company's data is isolated from every other, and every action inside the system carries the name of whoever took it.

FAQ

Questions every owner asks.

How do employees clock in?
Three ways, your choice: scan the branch QR with a location check, geofence alone with one tap, or a manager records it manually. Set the method for the whole company, a branch, a department, or a single employee — the most specific rule wins.
Do you track an employee's location all day?
No. Location is read only at the moment of clocking in or out, to confirm the employee is inside their branch geofence. No background tracking, and no trail of their movement.
How do employees join?
Invite them with one join link or a QR code; each installs the Permedjat app on their phone and joins your company directly.
Can it run more than one branch?
Yes. Every branch has its own geofence and working hours, every department its own permissions — all managed from one record.
How is payroll calculated?
A payroll cycle gathers attendance, deductions, additions, and loans, and freezes the figures the moment it is approved — with reports exported to Excel and PDF.
What devices does it run on?
An employee app and a management app on Android and iOS, plus a full dashboard on the web for anyone who prefers a bigger screen.
